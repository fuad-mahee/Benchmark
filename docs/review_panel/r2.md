## (a) Overall assessment

RQ1 is the real contribution and it is well executed: the census anchor (2,537 of GlitchCleaner's 2,539) is a genuine anchor, the match-rule decomposition reproduces exactly from the archived generations (I recomputed 988 → 1002, delta +14), and the five-model rescaling table with its fairness paragraph (lines 163–170) is exemplary practice. Everything downstream of RQ1 is weaker than the document states. The headline RQ3 argument against GlitchProber is contradicted by the study's own corrected artefacts, already sitting in `results/`; every GlitchProber comparison is run under a prompt GlitchProber never specified, which is fatal given the paper's own finding that wording explains 99% of a 2.5x population swing; and three criticisms of the original authors are either already conceded in the papers or plainly false against the papers' text. The document is unusually honest about its own errors but has not finished propagating those corrections into its conclusions.
